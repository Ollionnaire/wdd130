CAP HAITI

Cap-Haïtien, often called "La Perle du Nord", is one of Haiti’s most beautiful and historic tourist cities. With its rich colonial architecture, cobblestone streets, and colorful buildings, it offers a unique charm that takes visitors back in time. From the iconic "Citadelle Laferrière" a UNESCO World Heritage Site to the stunning "Labadee beach", Cap-Haïtien blends culture, history, and natural beauty. Tourists are drawn to its vibrant markets, friendly locals, and delicious Creole cuisine. It’s not just a city; it’s a living museum, where every corner tells a story.
