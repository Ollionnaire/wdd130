History

In the early 1980s, a young man named Jak, his head full of dreams, returned home to Haiti after studying forestry in France.One day, he noticed people coming to the coastal areas on vacation, but there weren’t many activities available inland. Jak remembered the great "La Mapou" river, full of clear water and small rapids.He asked a cousin who knew how to repair inflatable boats, and together they decided to create "Rivyè Klè" — the first rafting company in the region.

Over the years, "Rivyè Klè" grew in popularity, attracting adventurers from all over the world. Jak's vision of sharing the thrill of whitewater rafting with others became a reality, and the company expanded its offerings to include guided tours, safety training, and eco-friendly practices to preserve the natural beauty of the rivers.
