History

In the early 1980s, a young man named Jak, his head full of dreams, returned home to Haiti after studying forestry in France. He loved the water, the mountains, and adventure.One day, he noticed people coming to the coastal areas on vacation, but there weren’t many activities available inland. Jak remembered the great "La Mapou" river, full of clear water and small rapids.He asked a cousin who knew how to repair inflatable boats, and together they decided to create "Rivyè Klè" — the first rafting company in the region.The first year was difficult. People were skeptical. "What is rafting? Is it only for foreigners?" Jak reached out to local schools, offering free short trips so children could learn to swim and respect the river.Over time, word spread. Tourists began to visit, and locals started to sign up. Jak also hired young people with disabilities, showing that they too could work as guides and repair equipment.
